# Wovio SaaS - Go-To-Market & Sales Strategy

## 1. The Target Audience (Niche)
- **Primary ICP (Ideal Customer Profile):** Commercial Solar Installers, Commercial Roofers, and High-End Landscaping Companies in the USA (specifically Texas, Florida, and California).
- **Pain Point:** They know they need SEO and organic content to get high-ticket B2B leads, but SEO agencies charge $2,000+/mo. They don't have time to write blogs themselves.
- **The Hook:** "Get a dedicated AI SEO Content Manager working 24/7 for the price of a cup of coffee per day ($99/mo)."

## 2. Cold Email Outreach (The Engine)
We will leverage our existing Antigravity skills (`lead-factory-usa` and `cold-email-copywriter`) to launch a highly aggressive, money-focused B2B cold email campaign.
- **Scraping:** Use Google Maps/Apollo to find Roofers and Solar companies WITH websites (They need a website to connect to Wovio).
- **The Angle:** "Hey [Name], I audited your website. You're losing commercial contracts to [Competitor] because they rank for 'Commercial Roof Replacement'. I built an AI tool that writes and publishes SEO articles to your site automatically for $99/mo. Can I send you a free article it wrote about your business?"
- **The Demo:** We run their URL through Wovio's Backend (Phase 2), generate a perfect article, and email it to them. "Here is what it wrote today. Want it to do this 3x a week automatically? Sign up here."

## 3. The "Agency / White-Label" Play
- Once we secure 10 direct clients, we pivot to Marketing Agencies.
- **The Hook for Agencies:** "You charge your clients $1,500/mo for SEO. Pay us $299/mo for an Agency Tier in Wovio, connect 10 of your clients, and let Wovio do 100% of the fulfillment while you keep the massive profit margins."

## 4. LinkedIn Ghostwriting (Founder Brand & Investors)
Use the `ghostwriter-linkedin` skill to post 3 times a week on LinkedIn. We divide the content into two distinct funnels:
- **Client Acquisition Funnel:** "How we built an AI Autopilot that replaces Junior SEO writers", "Why ChatGPT wrappers fail, and why workflow automation wins."
- **Investor & VC Funnel:** Posts demonstrating traction, architecture, and Total Addressable Market (TAM). 
  - *Post Type 1 (The Vision):* "Why Vertical SaaS is eating the AI world. How Wovio is automating the $80B SEO industry."
  - *Post Type 2 (The Metric):* "Building Wovio's MVP in X days. Why an ultra-lean architecture (Next.js + Python) allows us to operate at a 90% profit margin."
  - *Post Type 3 (The Ask):* "We just crossed our first 10 beta testers for Wovio. Raising a pre-seed round to scale our Python backend infrastructure. DMs are open."

## 5. Influencer Marketing (Tech & AI Creators)
Partnering with Tech and Web Development influencers is the fastest way to acquire thousands of users at once, but it must be done professionally to protect Wovio's premium brand image. We will use a tiered approach:

### Tier 1: Macro-Influencers (Paid Sponsorships)
Big creators do not work for "free accounts." Offering them affiliates upfront can look cheap. We will approach them ONLY after we have our first 20-50 paying customers, using that revenue to pay for official, professional integrations.
- **Target Profiles:** YouTubers with 100k+ subs (e.g., *Matt Wolfe*, *Chris Do*, *Kevin Powell*).
- **The Pitch:** "We have a marketing budget of $X for a 60-second integration in your next AI tools video. We'd love to sponsor you."

### Tier 2: Micro-Influencers & Niche Creators
- **Target Profiles:** Up-and-coming YouTubers or Twitter creators (1k - 15k followers) talking specifically about "Webflow SEO" or "Local Business Marketing".
- **The Pitch:** Because they are hungry for content and growing, we offer them early VIP access to Wovio and a lucrative 30% recurring affiliate commission for their audience.
- **Initial Candidate Shortlist (YouTube):**
  1. *Matt Wolfe* (General AI Tools & Tech)
  2. *Chris Do / The Futur* (Creative & Agency Business Models - Perfect for our Agency Tier)
  3. *Kevin Powell* or *Finsweet* (Web Development space - to push Wovio as an add-on they can sell to their web clients).
  4. *Relume / Webflow focused channels* (Since Wovio pairs perfectly with new website builds).

## 6. General Social Media (Twitter/X & TikTok)
- **Twitter/X (Build in Public):** Share screenshots of the Dashboard, revenue updates, and technical Python/Next.js challenges. The "Build in Public" community is highly supportive and often yields early adopters.
- **TikTok/Reels:** Short form videos showing the "Aha! Moment": 
  - *Video concept:* "Watch me generate a month of blog posts in 30 seconds." Show the Wovio Drawer opening, the AI prompt box, and clicking "Publish".
